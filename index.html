                    Privacy notice

                    AlumniHub stores your name, course and batch, Google email and profile picture, your posts and comments, and the details you give in document requests (student number, course, year and purpose). It is used only to run the alumni community and to process your requests. Posts, comments and announcements are public. Document requests can be seen only by you and the admins. To correct or remove your information, contact the AlumniHub admin.
